What artists already do, and four gaps in the plan

We looked at six existing retopology tools to see what artists are used to. Four are manual or patch-based. Quad Remesher and Instant Meshes are automatic, and Mobius's planned guidance already goes beyond both. Four workflows that artists rely on are missing or unclear in the plan. Each needs your call before the specs change. Every card has a recommendation already selected, so change only the ones you disagree with.

What artists will expect from Mobius

  • Strokes and guide lines map to our direction strokes.
  • Contours and loops map to our surface curves, which force an edge loop.
  • TopoGun Mask maps to our density painting and region multipliers.
  • Symmetry on one key.
  • Snap to surface and a relax brush for touch-ups. Blender and RetopoFlow already do this on our output, so Mobius need not rebuild it.

Four decisions

Gaps in the plan

Decision 2 depends on decision 1. If you choose A for decision 1, option B of decision 2 is not available.

Decision 1 of 4

Can an artist give Mobius a hand-made partial low-poly and have it filled in?

The idea

The usual pro workflow is to build the critical loops by hand (eyes, mouth, shoulders, the areas that deform) and let a tool fill the rest. TopoGun's Patch works this way. A Nitro Retopo reviewer asked for a mode that "iterates on a mesh external to the base model".

What the plan says now. The region constraint has a "keep existing topology" override:

keep existing topology (copy the source faces unchanged and stitch them conformingly to the surrounding output)guidance-constraints spec, region overrides

The catch: this copies faces of the source mesh (the high-poly or sculpt). It helps when the input already has a clean hand. It cannot take a separate low-poly piece that the artist built in RetopoFlow or by hand.

Hand-made loops stay exactly as drawn; Mobius fills and stitches the rest.

Recommendation: Option B. It matches how artists already work with TopoGun and RetopoFlow, and the core machinery is already planned. C can still be offered later as a lighter mode.

Decision 2 of 4

Should hand fixes survive a re-run with changed guidance?

The idea

After any automatic run, artists touch up a few spots by hand. Then they want to change guidance somewhere else and run again without losing those fixes.

What the plan says now. The design lists this non-goal:

Editing the output mesh interactively after a run. Users re-run with changed guidance instead.design.md, Non-Goals

So today a re-run replaces the whole result, and hand edits are lost.

Recommendation: Option B. Side note, not a decision: TopoGun's Morpher handles a different case, the sculpt changing under a finished low-poly. That is out of scope here.

Decision 3 of 4

What should Mobius do with parts that are fused into one surface?

The idea

AI-generated meshes (which the proposal names as a target input) often weld separate objects into one connected surface: a sword melted into a hand, a belt fused into a torso. Retopologising across the fusion gives quads that flow straight through the junction. Nitro Retopo's main pitch is splitting these apart.

What the plan says now. mesh-core handles meshes with several disconnected components (each is retopologised independently) and reports problems. Nothing handles parts that are fused into one surface.

Recommendation: B now, and record C as a planned follow-up operation.

Decision 4 of 4

Should Mobius use the sharp edges, face sets, materials and seams an artist already set up in Blender?

The idea

Most of Quad Remesher's guides come from data that is already on the mesh: material borders, face sets, sharp edges and split normals. Artists who use it have learned to prepare meshes this way, and many sculpts, CAD imports and booleans arrive with sharp edges or face sets already set.

What the plan says now. The addon has its own tools for this:

The addon SHALL let users mark or exclude feature edges, assign faces to named regions with overrides (including deformation zones and "keep existing topology"), and draw surface boundary curves.blender-addon spec, feature, region and boundary authoring

Nothing reads Blender's own sharp edges, face sets, materials or UV seams. The engine does detect sharp edges by angle on its own, like Quad Remesher's "Detect Hard Edges by angle".

Recommendation: Option B. It gives Quad Remesher users the head start they expect while keeping every input visible and editable in one place.

Out of this page

Not decided here

  • A Nitro and Sketch-Retopo style "patch layout mode", where you choose edge counts or a pattern per patch along surface curves. It could come later on top of surface curves.
  • TopoGun's Morpher (the sculpt changes under a finished low-poly).
  • Map baking. It is already a planned later operation.
  • A Quad-Remesher-style switch between hitting the exact face count and following the shape ("Adapt Quad Count"). It belongs with the face-count tolerance decision in Module 6.
